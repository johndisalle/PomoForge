Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

The short version: PomoForge is built with privacy at its core. Your data stays on your device. We don't collect, sell, or share your personal information. We don't have servers. We don't track you.

1. Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. PomoForge is a fully offline application. All your data — workflows, session history, settings, and preferences — is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework.

2. iCloud Sync (Optional)

If you choose to enable iCloud Sync in Settings, your workflow and session data will be synced through Apple's CloudKit service. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

3. Family Focus Sharing (Optional)

If you enable Family Focus Sharing (Pro feature), your active focus session status (workflow name, interval type, and remaining time) may be shared with family members through CloudKit. No personal messages, location data, or other sensitive information is shared.

4. Subscriptions

PomoForge Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives:

  • An anonymous user identifier (not linked to your Apple ID or personal identity)
  • Subscription status (active/expired)
  • Transaction receipts (processed by Apple)

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or any personal information. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for more details.

5. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any analytics services. No Google Analytics, no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no tracking pixels, no third-party SDKs beyond RevenueCat for subscription management. We have no idea how you use the app, and that's by design.

6. Notifications

PomoForge may send local notifications for timer intervals, daily reminders, and streak alerts. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and are never sent through our servers (we don't have servers). You can manage notification permissions in iOS Settings.

7. Data Storage and Security

  • Local storage: All app data is stored in your device's sandboxed app container using Core Data, protected by iOS's built-in encryption.
  • No external servers: We do not operate servers, databases, or cloud infrastructure of any kind.
  • No network calls: The app makes no network requests except for iCloud sync (when enabled) and App Store subscription verification.

8. Children's Privacy

PomoForge is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since we collect no data at all, this is inherently satisfied.

9. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored on your device, you can delete all PomoForge data at any time by:

  • Deleting the app from your device (removes all local data)
  • Disabling iCloud Sync and deleting iCloud data through iOS Settings > Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Storage

10. Third-Party Services

PomoForge uses only the following third-party services:

  • Apple App Store: For app distribution and subscription payment processing
  • Apple CloudKit: For optional iCloud sync (user-initiated)
  • RevenueCat: For subscription management (anonymous identifiers only)

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@pomoforge.com or visit our Support page.
