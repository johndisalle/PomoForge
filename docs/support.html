Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a custom workflow?

Go to the Workflows tab, tap the + button in the top right, then add focus and break intervals. You can adjust durations, reorder intervals by dragging, and use quick templates. Tap Save when you're done.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open your iPhone Settings > tap your name at the top > Subscriptions > PomoForge > Cancel Subscription. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your billing period.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find PomoForge, and request a refund.

My timer isn't counting down. What do I do?

Try these steps: (1) Force quit the app and reopen it. (2) Make sure you have a workflow selected — tap the workflow name at the top of the Timer screen. (3) If the issue persists, delete and reinstall the app.

I'm not getting notifications when intervals end.

Go to iPhone Settings > Notifications > PomoForge and make sure notifications are enabled. Also check that "Time Sensitive" notifications are allowed, and that Focus Mode isn't blocking them.

How does the streak work?

Your streak counts consecutive days where you completed at least one focus session. Complete a session every day to keep your streak alive. You'll get a reminder at 8 PM if you haven't focused yet.

How do I export my session data?

Go to the History tab, tap the share icon in the top right, and your sessions will be exported as a CSV file. This is a Pro feature.

Does PomoForge work offline?

Yes. PomoForge is fully offline by default. The timer, workflows, history, and all features work without an internet connection. iCloud Sync is the only feature that requires connectivity, and it's optional.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All your data stays on your device. We don't collect any personal information, we don't have servers, and we don't track anything. Read our full Privacy Policy.

How do I restore my Pro subscription on a new device?

Open PomoForge, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used to purchase.
